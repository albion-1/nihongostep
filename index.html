Vocabulary mastery

Recognition is only the beginning. Assisted practice can make a word familiar; no-help recall makes it learned; using it correctly in a sentence makes it mastered.

No-help vocabulary practice

Prove you know the word

No answer choices. Recall checks whether the word comes to mind without help. Sentence use checks whether you can retrieve the right word from meaning and context.

Writing system

Kana mastery

Kana is learned a little at a time. The drill remembers each character you know, brings weak ones back more often, and still checks mastered characters occasionally.

No-help kana practice

Prove you know it

There are no romaji hints and no reference chart on this screen. Familiar kana become learned after two correct single recalls. A kana becomes mastered once it also has three correct appearances in three-at-a-time recall. Both drills keep cycling kana after those milestones.

Reading system · begins in Unit 3

100 beginner kanji

Kanji is learned through useful words, not giant reading tables. Each unit from 3 through 12 introduces 10 characters. The reading shown here is the reading used in the example word.

How mastery works: assisted recognition can make a kanji familiar. To become learned, you must recall its core meaning and read its example word without help. To become mastered, you must read that word again and recall the word's meaning without furigana.
No-help kanji practice

Prove you can read it

No reading table is shown here. The goal is not to recite every possible reading of a character; it is to recognize the kanji and read the word you actually learned it in.

Nihongo Step · v0.1 beta

About & settings

This beta is intentionally narrow: finish the first two units well before the course expands further.

What is in v0.1

Unit 1: first Japanese sentences, basic verb forms, existence, adjectives, wants, and the first ten hiragana.

Unit 2: a full core-particle unit covering は, が, を, に, で, へ, と, の, も, から, and まで.

Vocabulary mastery, kana mastery, adaptive review, lesson checks, and required unit finals are included.

Coming later

Units 3–13 are visible on the roadmap but are not part of this beta yet.

Kanji begins with Unit 3. The kanji system is already under development, but it is hidden from v0.1 learners until that unit exists.

Listening, speaking, account/cloud sync, and intermediate material are later additions.
